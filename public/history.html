    What am I looking at? +

    A record of each update

    Each point is the market median Kyara reported when a collection completed. Successful scheduled runs add points about every 30 minutes; manual runs add points too. Unchanged prices are still recorded.

    Window versus price metric

    24 hours, 7 days and 30 days choose how far back the chart looks. “Price tracked” chooses which rolling market median to plot. These are observations of those medians, not individual sales or a new median calculated for the chart window.

    Honest gaps and fresh starts

    History starts when this feature is enabled; earlier observations cannot be reconstructed. The collector keeps a rolling 30 days. Missing prices and collection gaps over 90 minutes break the line. The vertical axis scales to the observed prices.

    Dates and times use your device’s timezone.
