How the comparison works +

Mean of card medians

For the selected 7- or 30-day window, add the market median of each Mythical in a booster and divide by the number with a known price. Every eligible card receives equal weight. The middle card’s price is a separate statistic shown below each average.

Missing prices are excluded, never treated as zero. Coverage includes every card in the booster’s Mythical pool; a recommendation is withheld if either pool has missing prices.

What this tells you

The higher average favors a booster on Mythical prices alone, assuming equal Mythical drop odds between boosters and equal likelihood of each Mythical within its pool. Those within-rarity weights have not been verified.

This is not a full booster return calculation. It excludes other rarities, fees, special variants, and how quickly a card sells. Historical medians do not guarantee a future sale price.

Sources and freshness

The collector uses Kyara’s public website endpoints to discover both pools and read card prices. They are undocumented and may change. The bundled starter snapshot was collected separately from the website.

This page checks your hosted snapshot every five minutes while visible. “Check for updates” reads that saved snapshot; it does not request a new collection from Kyara. Collection runs on your configured schedule.
